DEPED CURRICULUMS

The Department of Education (DepEd) in the Philippines has introduced the MATATAG Curriculum, which aims to strengthen foundational education for learners.

Junior High School ( JHS)

The DepEd K12 Curriculum for Junior High School is part of a comprehensive education framework in the Philippines, encompassing Kindergarten and 12 years of basic education aimed at preparing students for various post-secondary pathways including higher education, technical training, and entrepreneurship.

Senior High School ( SHS )

Copilot Search Branding Like Dislike The Strengthened SHS Program streamlines the curriculum into two main tracks—Academic and Technical-Professional (TechPro)—with five core subjects and flexible electives to prepare students for higher education, employment, entrepreneurship, and technical skills.

Special Curricular Programs ( SCPs )

The Department of Education's Special Curricular Programs (SCPs) are purpose-designed educational initiatives within the Philippine high school system that aim to provide learners with specialized academic and co-curricular pathways aligned with their individual interests, aptitudes, and potential career trajectories. Institutionalized under DepEd Memorandum No. 149, s. 2011.
